# Coup · Corte Kiwi — original art

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Full-bleed portraits are compressed to WebP for the app. No original Coup artwork is reused.

Shared prompt: “Use case: stylized-concept. Original portrait for a Coup-themed digital card in a cohesive five-role kiwi bird court set. A charismatic round brown KIWI BIRD with a long slender beak, fluffy feathers and expressive dark eyes. Painterly premium board game illustration, lavish embroidered costumes, detailed brushwork, warm cinematic light, jewel tones. Centered three-quarter portrait, 2:3 vertical, softly blurred palace arch and botanical accents. No text, numbers, card borders, logos or watermarks. Original character, do not copy existing Coup art.”

- `duke.webp`: royal purple velvet cloak, gold leaf crown, embroidered collar and gold coins; deep plum palace.
- `assassin.webp`: crimson hood, charcoal velvet cloak, ceremonial curved dagger, ruby accents; smoky burgundy palace. No blood or violence.
- `captain.webp`: navy and teal brocade naval coat, gold epaulettes, tricorn hat and coin pouch; moonlit harbor.
- `ambassador.webp`: jade and turquoise ceremonial robes, formal feathered cap, two sealed ivory letters; embassy garden.
- `contessa.webp`: rose and ivory court gown, jeweled gold tiara and lace fan; rose marble palace.
