# Exploding Kiwi — direção das artes

As imagens em `public/assets/exploding-kiwi/` são ilustrações originais para o tema do Ford Kall. Não reproduzem logotipos, molduras, personagens ou artes oficiais de Exploding Kittens.

## Direção visual compartilhada

> Polished 2D editorial game illustration, playful original kiwi-fruit mascot, bold dark hand-inked outlines, soft screen-printed grain, expressive pose, compact centered composition, warm cream and leafy green palette with restrained coral accents, subtle paper texture. No text, letters, logos, card frame, watermark, or recognizable copyrighted character. Transparent or simple uncluttered background, square composition.

## Artes

- `kiwi-burst.png` — A round kiwi-fruit mascot shaped like a comically unstable cartoon bomb, tiny fuse sparkling, a small burst of leaves and harmless confetti; funny and mischievous rather than violent.
- `leaf-helmet.png` — The kiwi mascot confidently wearing an oversized layered leaf helmet as improvised protective gear, with a few loose leaves floating around.
- `skip-nap.png` — The kiwi mascot curled up in a tiny hammock for a quick nap, peaceful sleepy expression, one leaf used as a blanket.
- `double-turn.png` — Two energetic kiwi mascots racing around one another in a looping circle, conveying an extra turn with motion lines and playful urgency.
- `spyglass.png` — The kiwi mascot peeking through a brass spyglass at a small fan of blank, abstract cards, curious expression; no symbols or readable marks.
- `shuffle.png` — The kiwi mascot gleefully tumbling a small stack of blank cards into a swirl, with leaves and soft motion lines suggesting a reshuffle.
- `kiwi-gift.png` — The kiwi mascot presenting a small leafy gift box with a ribbon, friendly expression, simple celebratory sparkles.

These descriptions are the reusable generation prompts for the current asset set; the shared direction is intended to keep future card illustrations visually consistent.
